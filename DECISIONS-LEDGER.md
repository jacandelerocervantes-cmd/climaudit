# DECISIONS LEDGER — ClimAudit

Append-only record of every decision, with the reason, the evidence behind it, what was rejected, and who decided. Entries are never edited after the fact: a change is a **new entry** that says `Supersedes D-xxx`. Dates are 2026-10-16 unless stated.

**Who** values: `USER` = decided or confirmed by the user in chat; `ASSISTANT` = chosen by the assistant as a default and adopted unless the user changes it (these are the ones to challenge); `RULE` = imposed by an external rule or source.

**Status** values: `confirmed`, `adopted` (assistant default, not yet challenged), `open` (decision still needed), `superseded`.

Related documents: `RULES.md`, `PLAN.md`, `BACKEND-SPEC.md` (section 15 lists the 7 implementation decisions), `VERIFICATIONS.md`.
